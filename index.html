Why Trip Assistant?

From weekend escapes to journeys across the world, discover flights, hotels, holiday packages and more in one place. This independently built interface is a demonstration built as an independent travel-planning demo.

Booking Flights with Trip Assistant

Explore routes, compare sample fares, select your preferred cabin and plan a trip that suits you. The search, traveller details and booking confirmation flows in this demo work locally, without live inventory or real payments.

Domestic Flights on Trip Assistant

Discover popular routes between Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and other destinations. Choose one-way, round-trip or multi-city travel, and filter the demonstration results by airline, price and stops.
